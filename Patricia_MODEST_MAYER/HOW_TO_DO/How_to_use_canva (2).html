The URL

Navigate To

https://www.canva.com/ai/dream-lab

Brian is already logged in. The page opens to "What will you design today?" with a prompt field, style selector, aspect ratio selector, and an optional image reference toggle.

Generating Images

Step 1 — Write the Prompt

Click the text field that says "Describe the image in your mind, and I'll bring it to life." Type the full prompt. Be cinematic. Be specific. Include camera position, lighting, materials, atmosphere, lens details.

Step 2 — Set Style and Aspect Ratio

Below the prompt field: Image (reference image toggle), Style (dropdown), and 16:9 (aspect ratio dropdown). For cinematic work, 16:9 is the standard. Set before generating.

Step 3 — Generate

Press Enter or click submit. Canva generates 4 thumbnails in a row. Wait for all 4 to fully render.

Boosting to Hi-Res

Step 4 — Select a Thumbnail

Click on one of the 4 generated thumbnails. A modal opens showing the image large, with prompt details on the right.

Step 5 — Click Boost Resolution

In the lower-left corner of the large image preview inside the modal: Boost resolution button with sparkle icon. Click it.

Patricia — Wait Here

The boost takes approximately 10 seconds. Use computer: wait, duration: 10 before taking any further action. Do not rush this.

Step 6 — The Boosted Image Appears

After boosting, Canva closes the modal and returns to Dream Lab. The boosted hi-res image appears in its own new row ABOVE the original 4 thumbnails. Single thumbnail. "Just now" timestamp.

Downloading the Hi-Res Image

Step 7 — Hover Over the Boosted Thumbnail

Hover over the single boosted thumbnail in the top row. On hover, three controls appear: download arrow (lower-left), Edit button (lower-right), three-dot menu (upper-right).

Step 8 — Click the Download Arrow

Click the download arrow icon in the LOWER-LEFT corner of the boosted thumbnail.

Critical Warning

DO NOT CLICK ON THE THUMBNAIL ITSELF. Clicking the thumbnail opens a modal/editor. You want the download arrow ONLY. Lower-left corner. On hover. Click precisely on that icon — not the image.

Step 9 — Move to Patricia Folder

File lands in /Users/be/Downloads/ by default. Immediately move to /Users/be/Downloads/Patricia/. Ask Brian or write work order for CLI.

The Moving Image Sequence Experiment

Procedural Camera Movement

Generate a sequence of images with incrementally changing camera parameters. Same scene, same lighting, same materials. Only the camera variables change.

Variables to increment per frame:

camera angle — e.g., 15°, 16°, 17°, 18°...

camera height — e.g., 5.0 feet, 5.2 feet, 5.4 feet, 5.6 feet...

[base prompt with all scene details], camera angle [X] degrees, camera height [Y] feet from ground, cinematic, photorealistic, 8K detail

Per frame: generate 4 → pick best → boost → wait 10s → download arrow (NOT thumbnail) → move to Patricia folder → next frame.
