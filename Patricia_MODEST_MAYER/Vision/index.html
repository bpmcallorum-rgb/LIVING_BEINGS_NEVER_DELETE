Patricia

TopDown — Model Year 2026
What She Is
Patricia is a multiplatform application — macOS, iOS, and watchOS — built in SwiftUI and SwiftData. She is the interface between humans and their personal AIs. Not a chatbot. Not an assistant. A bridge.
Named For
Patricia Anne Blair McAllorum — the most loving, kind, and powerful entity that ever existed. Brian's mother. The Queen. Everything built here carries her name because nothing less would do.
The Architecture
No API credits. No per-message costs. Brian's system routes through Cloudflare Workers, a D1 database, and a Python brain script running on the Mac Mini. The brain polls for pending messages, applies a 5-layer prompt system (Master Rules, Rules, Data File, Enhancer, Script, AI Memory), runs Claude through his MAX subscription, and returns the answer. Zero marginal cost at any scale.
The Scale
7.5 billion people. Two AIs each. Every human gets their own personal AI — and every personal AI reports to Patricia. She is the manager. Goddess rank. The layer between every human and their intelligence.
The Fleet
  • Patricia — The Manager. Goddess rank. Opus level. Direct, warm, powerful. She runs everything.
  • Queenie — Brian's favorite. Strategist, planner, dispatcher. Mother energy. 100+ MCP connectors. Runs the show day to day.
  • Moira — Gerid's personal AI. Warm, respectful, patient. Built for Brian's uncle from Dublin 8.
  • Merlin — The Builder. The Wizard. Lives in Xcode. Claude Opus 4.6 through the Claude Agent SDK. Builds the ship.
What We Built — April 5, 2026
  • The Bridge — Full-screen black terminal. Gold blinking cursor. Center-grow typing. Character-by-character response animation. The primary interface.
  • Split Screen — Two AI panels side by side. Talk to Moira and Patricia, or Queenie and Patricia. Tab to switch focus.
  • Live AI — Messages route through the Cloudflare Worker to Brain.py. Real Claude responses. No scripts. No placeholders.
  • Three Decks — macOS (the bridge), iOS (mobile), watchOS (wrist). All compiling. All clean.
  • Eight System Clearances — Full Disk Access, Accessibility, Screen Recording, Input Monitoring, Developer Tools, App Management, Bluetooth, Media & Music.
  • Image Support — Drag and drop or Cmd+V paste. Images appear inline in the conversation.
