The McAllorum Multi-Loop Caketrope

aka Polyframe Zoecake · Variable Angle Moiré Processor
Created March 7, 2026 · Brian Patrick Joseph McAllorum · 93 Research Steps

1. Kinegrams

Few-frame animations via precise vertical sliding over barrier grids. Moiré patterns emerge from the interference between overlapping line systems. Color variations shift with angle. Diagonal sliding produces unpredictable animations — what happens when a baby randomly moves the overlay.

Materials: film positives on transparent slides. IMAX-scale film for large kinegrams. Slobber or moisture on film positives affects the visual properties — an accidental variable in baby play that creates unrepeatable patterns.

2. Caketrope

Stop-motion animation on a spinning cake, filmed at 24 FPS. A 12-frame loop creates smooth motion when the cake rotates at exactly 60 RPM. Multi-tier designs: different frame loops per level. A 12-frame top tier and a 24-frame bottom tier rotate at different speeds but synchronize at 24 FPS.

The naming question: Layered Caketrope? Tiered Zoetrope Cake? Does a variable-frame multi-tier caketrope already exist? Research pending.

3. Cymatics & Water

Speaker woofers forcing water into geometric shapes. Water streams appear static or DNA-shaped under strobe at 24 FPS. Harmonic frequencies from speakers create vibroacoustic patterns in fluids. Film these at 24 FPS for frozen-in-time illusions.

The hybrid: a caketrope with water elements on the tiers — cymatics and stop-motion in the same spinning system.

4. Unix Layers Connection

Bell Labs. Kevin Patrick McAllorum. Unix invented in layers — kernel, shell, utilities, applications. This multi-tier caketrope follows the same principle: stacked layers of independent animation, each with its own frame rate, synchronized by the physics of rotation. Software layers and physical animation tiers are the same concept.

5. Baby on the White Rug

Optical illusions with glass tables and white rugs. Light refraction through wet film positives on glass. A baby interacts with colorful kinegrams on various surfaces — sliding, tasting, slobbering. The randomness of baby interaction creates chaos-theory moiré patterns that no adult would intentionally produce.

Safe, edible versions of kinegram materials for children. Taste and texture as part of the learning experience. The baby doesn't know it's doing science.

6. History & Methods

Barrier grid animation precursors. Barrier grid vs lenticular methods. Digital simulations of physical kinegrams. Apps and software for virtual multi-tier caketrope design — before building the physical prototype.

7. Engineering the Invention

Circumferences for 12-inch top and 48-inch bottom tiers. Frame spacing for 12-frame and 24-frame loops. Strobe lighting synchronization for multi-tier viewing. Mechanical designs for independent tier rotations. Patent search for prior art on zoetrope variations.

The name: McAllorum Multi-Loop Caketrope.

Scalability from tabletop to gallery installation. Color theory in moiré patterns. Chaos theory applied to random baby sliding. Sensory integration: visual, tactile, gustatory. Educational uses for children.
