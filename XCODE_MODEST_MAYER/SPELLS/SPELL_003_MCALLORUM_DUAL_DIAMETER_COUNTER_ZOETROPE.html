1. Counter-Rotating Fundamentals

Two zoetropes spinning in opposite directions at 24 FPS. The mechanics of opposition: when the eye tracks the outer animation clockwise, the inner animation sweeps counter-clockwise, creating interference patterns that neither cylinder produces alone. Color shifts emerge from opposing rotation — blues lean purple, reds lean orange, the spectrum bends.

2. Double Diameter Design

The outer zoetrope has double the diameter of the inner one. Double the circumference means double the frames at the same FPS — 48 frames on the outer ring while the inner holds 24. The visual result: the outer animation moves at half the apparent speed of the inner, creating a foreground/background depth illusion that doesn't exist in any flat-plane animation.
