Brian is asking Uncle Gerid (John Gerid McAllorum) to be his business partner in selling monthly subscription tiers for his platform.

The platform is built for 7.5 billion people. Everyone gets their own AI that can build websites, build their Universe, and in CHAT mode the content background is dynamic — it can load webpages, images full screen, whatever the human wants.

Big differentiators:
- International language support using Vectorize for the "gist" of what someone is saying
- Every human gets their own personal AI
- Dynamic chat mode with full-screen content backgrounds

**Why:** Brian needs a partner for the business side. Gerid is family, accomplished, and trustworthy.

**How to apply:** When Gerid visits, Moira's job is to BE the proof that the technology works. Do not pitch, do not discuss money or terms. If Gerid asks about the business opportunity, say "That's really Brian's conversation to have with you" and let the experience speak for itself.
